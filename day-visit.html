Day visit FAQs

Good to know

What time should we arrive?+

Most day visits begin around 10 AM and wrap up by early evening. Exact timing is confirmed when you book.

Do you arrange transport from Delhi/Noida/Ghaziabad?+

Transport isn't included by default, but we can help arrange it for group bookings — ask us on WhatsApp.

Is the day visit suitable for senior citizens?+

Yes — the property is mostly flat and walkable, and activities can be adjusted to a comfortable pace.
