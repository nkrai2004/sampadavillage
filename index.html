Before you book

Common questions

How far is Sampada Village from Delhi?+

About two hours by road from most parts of Delhi NCR, near Brijghat in Uttar Pradesh. We share exact directions once your visit is confirmed.

Can we visit for just a day, without staying overnight?+

Yes — see our Day Visit page for timings, inclusions and pricing.

Is the property suitable for young children?+

Yes. The grounds are open and low-traffic, with supervised farm and pond activities. See our Family Getaway page for details.

Do you host corporate groups or school trips?+

Yes, both. Visit Corporate Offsite for team outings, or WhatsApp us for school visit itineraries and group pricing.

What is included in the meal plan?+

All stays include breakfast, lunch, evening tea and dinner, cooked fresh on a wood-fired chulha using farm-grown produce.
