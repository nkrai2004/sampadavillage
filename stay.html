Stay FAQs

Good to know

What is check-in and check-out time?+

Standard check-in is 1 PM and check-out is 11 AM. Early check-in or late check-out can be arranged on request, subject to availability.

How many people can stay in one hut?+

Each hut comfortably sleeps 2–3 guests. For larger families or groups, we recommend booking multiple huts.

Are meals customisable for dietary needs?+

Yes — let us know about vegetarian-only, Jain, or other preferences when you enquire, and we'll plan the menu accordingly.

Is there WiFi or mobile network at the property?+

Mobile network is available; the setting is intentionally low on distraction, so we'd suggest treating it as a break from screens where you can.
