The Cottages

Mud & Thatch Huts

Round-roofed rooms, raised by hand

Every cottage shares the same bones — mud-plastered walls, a steep thatch roof, and a porch you'll end up spending more time on than the bed. Interiors are simple and unfussy, dressed in bamboo and hand-block linen.

The Porch

Where mornings happen

A porch built for staying put

Bamboo posts hold up a deep thatch overhang, shading a cane sofa and a ring of wicker chairs — the obvious place for the first chai of the day, and the last conversation of the night.

  • Private, furnished porch per cottage
  • Climbing vines & potted plants at the entrance
  • Stone garden path to every door
The Room

Where you sleep

Simple, comfortable, unmistakably handmade

Bamboo four-poster beds, hand-block quilts, glass-topped bamboo side tables — nothing that couldn't have been made on the property, and mostly things that were.

  • Bamboo four-poster double bed
  • Attached bathroom with running hot water
  • Power backup & charging points
