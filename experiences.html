Around the Property

Ways to spend your hours here

Nothing here is scheduled too tightly — most days sort themselves out between the pond, the garden, and whatever's cooking.

Pond & Garden Walks

Wander the stone paths between marigold beds and vegetable rows, down to the pond where the resident swans keep watch over the evening.

Lantern-Lit Evenings

As the light fades, the paths are lit lantern by lantern — a good excuse for a slow walk before dinner.

Sunset Over the Fields

Step past the boundary and the mustard fields open right up — bring the whole family out for golden hour.

Bonfire & Deck Evenings

The painted pavilion at the centre of the property comes alive after dark — chairs pulled in, a fire lit, stories that run long.

Farm-to-Plate Meals

Thalis built around whatever the garden has that day, cooked to order and served hot on the communal deck.

Slow Mornings

No alarms required — chai on the porch, mist over the pond, and a day that starts whenever you're ready.

Celebrations

Festivals & get-togethers on the deck

The pavilion at Sampada regularly turns into a party ground — long tables, string lights, and the same thatch roof overhead, whatever the occasion.
